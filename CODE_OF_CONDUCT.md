# Community code of conduct

CapyBite welcomes respectful, constructive participation.

Participants must communicate professionally, respect different backgrounds and experience levels, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to [service.help@outlook.kr](mailto:service.help@outlook.kr).
